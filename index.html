Undergraduate thesis research at ITS Geophysical Engineering (GPA 3.41).   • Hasil Penelitian: Menghasilkan prediksi litologi yang sangat akurat serta evaluasi matriks konfusi dan profil perbandingan log prediksi vs aktual.
  • Hybrid Pipeline: Utilizing CRBM to extract complex non-linear feature representations from well logs and PSO to find optimal XGBoost hyperparameters.
  • Blind Well Testing: Validating lithology classification performance (Sandstone, Siltstone, Mudstone, Shale) on blind test wells without data leakage.

Menggunakan model XGBoost Classifier dengan hyperparameter tuning berbasis Optuna untuk mengklasifikasi 12 jenis litologi (Sandstone, Shale, Limestone, Marl, Dolomite, Chalk, Halite, Anhydrite, Tuff, Coal, Basement, Sandstone/Shale) dari 10 fitur well log (GR, RHOB, NPHI, DTC, DRHO, CALI, RDEP, RMED, ROP, RSHA). Automated lithology classification pipeline developed by Tim Energi Nusantara at the Scale Up Geoenergy Hackathon 2025. Utilized an XGBoost Classifier with Optuna-based hyperparameter optimization to classify 12 lithology types (Sandstone, Shale, Limestone, Marl, Dolomite, Chalk, Halite, Anhydrite, Tuff, Coal, Basement, Sandstone/Shale) from 10 well log features (GR, RHOB, NPHI, DTC, DRHO, CALI, RDEP, RMED, ROP, RSHA). Data was preprocessed through KNN Imputation, MinMax Scaling, Label Encoding, and spatial well-proximity clustering.

  • Akurasi Validasi 96%: Model XGBoost mencapai akurasi 96.04% pada data validasi (80/20 split) untuk 12 kelas litologi.
  • Skor F1 60%: Berhasil melakukan pelabelan litologi otomatis pada sumur blind test (16-2-6, 17-4-1, 25-10-10) dengan skor F1 60%.
  • Pengolahan Data: Pipeline preprocessing lengkap: pembacaan LAS multi-sumur, KNN Imputation untuk missing values, MinMax Scaling, dan Label Encoding.
  • Pemodelan Terklaster: Penerapan spatial clustering berbasis kedekatan antar sumur untuk mengurangi heterogenitas geologi dan meningkatkan akurasi prediksi per cluster.
  • Optuna Hyperparameter Tuning: Optimasi otomatis parameter XGBoost (n_estimators, max_depth, learning_rate, subsample, colsample_bytree, gamma, reg_alpha, reg_lambda).
  • 96% Validation Accuracy: XGBoost model achieved 96.04% accuracy on validation data (80/20 split) across 12 lithology classes.
  • 60% F1-Score: Successfully performed automated lithology labeling on blind test wells (16-2-6, 17-4-1, 25-10-10) achieving a 60% F1 score.
  • Data Pipeline: Full preprocessing pipeline: multi-well LAS reading, KNN Imputation for missing values, MinMax Scaling, and Label Encoding.
  • Spatial Clustering: Applied spatially clustered modeling based on well proximity to minimize geological heterogeneity and improve per-cluster prediction accuracy.
  • Optuna Hyperparameter Tuning: Automated optimization of XGBoost parameters (n_estimators, max_depth, learning_rate, subsample, colsample_bytree, gamma, reg_alpha, reg_lambda).
